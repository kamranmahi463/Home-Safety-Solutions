Painting in Muscat, Oman

A fresh coat of paint changes the whole feeling of a home. We prepare the walls, repair small cracks and apply paint carefully so that furniture and floors stay clean. We work in villas, apartments, offices and shops.

What is included

  • Interior wall and ceiling painting
  • Exterior wall painting
  • Crack filling and surface preparation
  • Door and window frame painting
  • Colour advice for your rooms
  • Cleaning the area after work

How the service works

  1. Contact us. Send a message on WhatsApp or use the contact form and describe your painting requirement.
  2. Site visit. Our team comes to your door in Muscat and checks the work.
  3. Price discussion. We explain the work and discuss the price with you after the site visit.
  4. Work and check. We complete the job, clean the area and test the result with you.

Why choose Home Safely Solutions

We have been serving homes and small businesses in Muscat since 2016. Our team arrives at your door, works neatly and respects your property. You never pay for work you have not approved, because the price is discussed with you before we start.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to move my furniture?

We help cover and move light items. For heavy furniture we protect it with sheets.

How long does a room take?

It depends on the size and condition of the walls. We tell you the expected time after the site visit.

Who chooses the paint?

You choose the colour and brand, and we can suggest options if you want advice.
