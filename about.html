About Us – Our Story

Home Safely Solutions started in 2016 in Al Amarat, Muscat, with one simple goal: make home maintenance easy, honest and reliable. What began with small repair jobs has grown into a wider home service covering plumbing, electrical work, air conditioning, painting, carpentry, CCTV cameras, video door bells, WiFi and door closers.

The business is handled by Kamran Khan. Over the years we have learned that most customers want three things: a quick reply, a team that arrives and does the job properly, and a fair price they understand before work begins.

How we work

We come to your door, inspect the work and discuss the price after the site visit. We keep the work area clean, explain what we did, and stay in touch on WhatsApp if you need help later.

Our promise

  • Honest advice about what is really needed
  • Neat and careful work
  • Respect for your home and time
  • Price discussed before the work starts
