Home maintenance you can rely on

Home Safely Solutions is a home maintenance service based in Al Amarat, Muscat. Since 2016 we have helped families, tenants and small businesses with plumbing, electrical work, air conditioning, painting, carpentry, CCTV cameras, video door bells, WiFi and many small household jobs.

Every job starts with a simple step: we visit your property, look at the problem and explain what is needed. Only after the site visit do we discuss the price, so there are no surprises.
